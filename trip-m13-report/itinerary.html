What this plan does not know

stated rather than smoothed over

Every price is unknown — which is not the same as free

All seven stops carry no recorded admission. The trip total is therefore unknown, not zero, and any figure you see elsewhere is a floor rather than an estimate. The video never quoted a per-place price.

No opening hours, anywhere

Seven of seven stops were placed without knowing when they open. The times above are where the planner put them, not when the doors are unlocked. Hongya Cave in particular is a night sight scheduled here at ten in the morning. Verify before you go.

Transit times are geometry, not a timetable

The rail graph is real — 38 route relations, 288 stations, 692 links, including the monorail a naive query misses. But OpenStreetMap carries no interval, headway or duration tag for any city this was measured in, so line speed and the boarding allowance are assumed and actual waiting depends on a frequency nothing here knows.

The day is mostly empty, and that is honest

Fourteen places were named in the video; seven became stops. Four were rejected as unreachable, three could not be resolved. The planner leaves the gap visible instead of padding it with places nobody mentioned.
